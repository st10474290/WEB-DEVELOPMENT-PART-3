About Trecia's Grill

Pretoria, South Africa

Who We Are

Trecia's Grill is a modern African-inspired restaurant established in Pretoria in 2020. We serve freshly prepared grilled meals and familiar South African favourites in a welcoming environment.

Customers can dine in, order takeaway meals or arrange catering for special events.

Mission

To serve fresh, quality meals with excellent service and good value.

Vision

To become a recognised casual-dining restaurant known for great food and African hospitality.

Target Audience

Families, event planners, corporate clients, schools, NGOs and private celebrations.

Dietary Options

Selected vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free and halal options are available on request.
